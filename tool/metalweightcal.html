Metal Weight Calculator

A fast and simple Windows app to calculate the weight of any metal by simply entering dimensions. Free and works offline.

🔧 App Features

Important Note: The Metal Weight Calculator is a standalone Windows app — it works independently of the HEWP Excel Tool and does not require Excel or any browser extension.

Multiple Shapes

Calculate weight for round, sheet, tube, rectangle, and many other metal shapes.

Flexible Units

Use any combination of metric (mm/cm/m/kg) or imperial (inch/ft/lbs) units for input and output.

Multiple Metals

Supports steel, aluminum, cast iron, nickel, copper, brass, gold, silver, and many more.

Works Offline

No internet connection required. All calculations are performed locally on your device.

Fast & Simple

Clean, intuitive interface designed for quick and accurate calculations with zero learning curve.

Cost & Estimate

Set and save price per kg or lb for each metal — get total cost and generate estimates instantly.
